SERVICES

Power and electronics for offshore-capable vessels.

✦Lithium Battery Retrofits

Drop-in and fully engineered LiFePO4 conversions — sizing, BMS integration, fusing, and wiring done to marine standards.

✦Inverter Retrofits

Inverter/charger upgrades (Victron and others) for clean, reliable AC power underway and at anchor.

✦Solar Panel Integration

Rigid and flexible arrays on arches, biminis, and decks — MPPT controllers sized and wired for real-world output.

✦Wind Generator Installation

Marine wind turbines mounted and wired for quiet, dependable charging on passage.

✦Hydro Generator Installation

Tow-behind and transom-mounted hydro generators for serious amp-hours on long passages.

✦Alternator Regulator Upgrades

External smart regulation so your engine actually finishes the charge — faster, fuller, safer.

✦Generator Installation

Marine generator installs and integration with new or existing power systems.

✦Stray Current Evaluation

✦Complex Problem Solving

The weird electrical gremlins nobody else wants to touch. Systematic diagnosis, honest answers.
